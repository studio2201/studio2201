# Section 0 · One question per product



| Product | The one question |
|---|---|
| **Vigil** | "How much of my project's risk comes from dead libraries?" |
| **Proven** | "Is this published artifact really what its source says?" |
| **Aegis** | "Which lines are keeping me RSA-encrypted past 2030?" |
| **Snip** | "Did the AI leaky-anything it shouldn't have?" |
| **Boneyard** | "Which of my org's repos are quietly dying?" |

If a service can be described without invoking its primary question, it's doing too much. Strip it.
